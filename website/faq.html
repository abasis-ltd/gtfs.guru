GTFS guides

GTFS (General Transit Feed Specification) is the zip of CSV files that transit agencies publish so trip planners like Google Maps and Apple Maps can show their routes, stops and timetables. These guides cover the whole path: what the format is, how to build a feed, how to check it, and how to get it accepted and keep it current.

New to GTFS? Start with what GTFS is used for, then build a minimal feed and validate it. Already publishing and got rejected? Go to why GTFS feeds get rejected.
