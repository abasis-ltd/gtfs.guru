Diagnosis

What this notice means

The rule does not say how far in advance a same-day booking is required.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Passengers may attempt to book too late.

Scope

Files to check and report fields

  • booking_rules.txtOpen GTFS specification
Report fieldTypeMeaning
bookingRuleIdstringThe `booking_rules.booking_rule_id` of the faulty record.
csvRowNumberintegerThe row number of the faulty record.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Check the reported booking rule against the operator's minimum lead time.

  2. 2

    Set prior_notice_duration_min in minutes. If a maximum is supplied, ensure it is not smaller than the minimum.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.

Example correction

After
