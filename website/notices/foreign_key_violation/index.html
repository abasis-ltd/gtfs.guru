Diagnosis

What this notice means

The value in childFieldName cannot be found in the referenced file and field named by the report.

Operational effect

Why it matters

An app may reject or omit the affected stop, trip, route, or other record.

Scope

Files to check and report fields

Use the file or input resource identified in the report. Check the message and available context fields; runtime failures may not identify a GTFS row.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Open childFilename at csvRowNumber, then search for the exact fieldValue in parentFilename and parentFieldName.

  2. 2

    Correct a mistyped reference, restore the real missing record, or remove an obsolete record and its dependencies. Check case and whitespace. For service IDs, consider both calendar files; never invent a placeholder stop or service.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
