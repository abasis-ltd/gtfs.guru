Diagnosis

What this notice means

The reported translation field is populated where the selected table or targeting method forbids it.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The translation may identify its target in contradictory ways.

Scope

Files to check and report fields

  • translations.txtOpen GTFS specification
Report fieldTypeMeaning
csvRowNumberintegerThe row number of the faulty record.
fieldNamestringThe name of the field that was expected to be empty.
fieldValuestringActual value of the field that was expected to be empty.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Inspect fieldName and the complete row in translations.txt.

  2. 2

    Keep the permitted targeting fields for that table and clear the conflicting value. Check the distinction between record-based and field_value-based targeting.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
