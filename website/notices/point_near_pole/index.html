Diagnosis

What this notice means

The reported point lies near the North or South Pole.

Operational effect

Why it matters

A sign, unit, or coordinate-order error may place the point far from the service area.

Scope

Use the file or input resource identified in the report. Check the message and available context fields; runtime failures may not identify a GTFS row.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Check the original latitude and longitude, their signs, and coordinate system.

  2. 2

    Export the correct WGS84 decimal-degree coordinates. Verify truly unusual locations rather than forcing them into an arbitrary range.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
