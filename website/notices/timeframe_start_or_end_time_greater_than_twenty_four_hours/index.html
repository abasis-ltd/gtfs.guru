Diagnosis

What this notice means

start_time or end_time in timeframes.txt exceeds the permitted local-day limit.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Fare timeframes use local calendar-day times rather than extended trip service-day times.

Scope

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Inspect the reported time and the dates on which the fare applies.

  2. 2

    Split an overnight interval into valid local-day periods, such as 22:00:00–24:00:00 and 00:00:00–02:00:00, with the correct service dates for each. Do not copy extended stop_times.txt times here.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
