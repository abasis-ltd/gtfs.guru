Diagnosis

What this notice means

The trip's final shape_dist_traveled exceeds the shape's maximum distance, and the endpoint separation exceeds the implementation's 11.1 m threshold.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The stop distances and route geometry do not describe the same endpoint reliably.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Compare maxTripDistanceTraveled, maxShapeDistanceTraveled, the units, and the reported endpoint separation.

  2. 2

    Correct the trip's shape assignment, extend an incomplete shape to the real endpoint, or recompute distances from accurate geometry. Do not just increase the shape's final number.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
