Operational effect

Why it matters

Journey planners may show impossible travel times; the issue often reveals swapped coordinates or an incorrect timestamp.

Repair

How to fix it

Verify both stop coordinates and the consecutive arrival/departure times. Correct the value that creates the unrealistic speed.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open stop_times.txt and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Verify both stop coordinates and the consecutive arrival/departure times. Correct the value that creates the unrealistic speed.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that fast_travel_between_consecutive_stops no longer appears for the record.
