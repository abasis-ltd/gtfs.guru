Diagnosis

What this notice means

The distance and elapsed time between consecutive stops imply a speed above the rule's threshold for this transport type.

Operational effect

Why it matters

A wrong stop location or time can produce misleading journey durations.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Compare the reported stop IDs, coordinates, departure time, arrival time, and speedKph with the actual journey.

  2. 2

    Correct the mistaken coordinate, stop reference, time, or route type. For travel after midnight, preserve the service-day time, such as 25:10:00. Keep verified unusual services unchanged.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
