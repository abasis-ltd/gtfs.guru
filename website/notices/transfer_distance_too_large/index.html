Diagnosis

What this notice means

The reported transfer spans a very large distance.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The transfer may link the wrong stops or suggest an impractical connection.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Compare the stop IDs and coordinates with the intended interchange.

  2. 2

    Correct the mistaken reference or location. If the connection is real, verify that its transfer type and timing describe what passengers actually do.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
