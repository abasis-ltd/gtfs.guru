Diagnosis

What this notice means

A timed trip's first or last stop lacks arrival_time or departure_time.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Apps cannot reliably determine when the trip starts or ends.

Scope

Files to check and report fields

  • stop_times.txtOpen GTFS specification
Report fieldTypeMeaning
csvRowNumberintegerThe row of the faulty record.
specifiedFieldstringName of the missing field.
stopSequenceinteger`stops.stop_sequence` of the faulty record.
tripIdstringThe `trips.trip_id` of the faulty record.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Find the reported trip and sort its stop_times.txt rows by stop_sequence.

  2. 2

    Supply both times at the affected boundary from the timetable. When arrival and departure are the same event, use the same value for both; preserve hours above 24 for overnight service.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
