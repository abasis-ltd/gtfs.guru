Diagnosis

What this notice means

The reported number is invalid under the country context used by the validator.

Operational effect

Why it matters

A passenger contact number may be unusable, or the validation country may be wrong.

Scope

Files to check and report fields

Use the file or input resource identified in the report. Check the message and available context fields; runtime failures may not identify a GTFS row.

Report fieldTypeMeaning
This notice has no structured context fields.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Compare the number with the operator's published contact and check the country supplied for validation.

  2. 2

    Correct an erroneous number or country context. Preserve a verified valid contact number rather than replacing it solely to satisfy an incorrect regional assumption.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
