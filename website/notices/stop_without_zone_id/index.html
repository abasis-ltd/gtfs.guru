Diagnosis

What this notice means

The stop is used by a route with zone-dependent legacy fare rules, but its zone_id is empty.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Apps may be unable to determine the applicable fare.

Scope

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Trace the stop's trips and routes to the relevant fare_rules.txt rows.

  2. 2

    Assign its actual fare zone in stops.txt, consistent with the fare rules. Do not assign every stop to one zone merely to fill the field.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
