Diagnosis

What this notice means

Stops.stop_access is forbidden for locations that are stations, entrances, generic nodes or boarding areas. It can only be specific when a stop is associated with a parent station.

Operational effect

Why it matters

This is a GTFS requirement violation. Consumers may reject the affected data or interpret it incorrectly.
