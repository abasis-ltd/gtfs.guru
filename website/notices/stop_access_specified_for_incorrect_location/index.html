Diagnosis

What this notice means

stop_access is populated on a station, entrance, generic node, or boarding area.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The flag does not apply to that kind of location.

Scope

Files to check and report fields

  • stops.txtOpen GTFS specification
Report fieldTypeMeaning
This notice has no structured context fields.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Inspect the reported row's location_type in stops.txt.

  2. 2

    Clear stop_access where forbidden, or correct a wrongly classified location. Use the flag only on an eligible stop/platform with a parent station.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
