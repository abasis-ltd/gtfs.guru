Diagnosis

What this notice means

Trip frequencies must not overlap in time. Two entries X and Y are considered to directly overlap if X.start_time is less than or equal to Y.start_time and Y.start_time is less than X.end_time.

Operational effect

Why it matters

This is a GTFS requirement violation. Consumers may reject the affected data or interpret it incorrectly.
