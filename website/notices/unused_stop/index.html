Diagnosis

What this notice means

The stop is not referenced by stop_times.txt and is not an ancestor of a referenced stop. The thorough validator mode reports this warning.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The feed carries a location that is not served and is not needed as a parent, increasing noise and the chance of stale data.

Repair

How to fix it

Remove the stop if obsolete, or reference it from the correct stop_times row. This notice is produced in thorough mode.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open stops.txt / stop_times.txt and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Remove the stop if obsolete, or reference it from the correct stop_times row. This notice is produced in thorough mode.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that unused_stop no longer appears for the record.

Worked example

Bad row and correction

Before

stops.txt: S2,Unused Stop,35.18,33.37
stop_times.txt: no row references S2

After

stop_times.txt: T1,09:10:00,09:10:00,S2,2

Simplified example for stops.txt / stop_times.txt; keep all other required columns from your feed.
