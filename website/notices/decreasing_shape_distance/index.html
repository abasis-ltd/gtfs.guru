Diagnosis

What this notice means

When sorted by shape.shape_pt_sequence, two consecutive shape points must not have decreasing values for shape_dist_traveled.

Operational effect

Why it matters

This is a GTFS requirement violation. Consumers may reject the affected data or interpret it incorrectly.
