A stop in stops.txt is not referenced by any stop_times.stop_id.

Diagnosis

What this notice means

Such stops are not used by any trip and normally do not provide user value. This notice may indicate a typo in stop_times.txt.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The stop is never served and can clutter maps or indicate a misspelled stop_id in stop_times.txt.

Repair

How to fix it

Reference the stop from the intended trip, or remove the stops.txt row if the location is no longer published.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open stops.txt / stop_times.txt and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Reference the stop from the intended trip, or remove the stops.txt row if the location is no longer published.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that stop_without_stop_time no longer appears for the record.

Worked example

Bad row and correction

Before

stops.txt: S_UNUSED,Old Terminal,35.17,33.36
stop_times.txt: no row references S_UNUSED

After

stops.txt: S1,Terminal,35.17,33.36
stop_times.txt: T1,08:00:00,08:00:00,S1,1
