Diagnosis

What this notice means

The stop is not referenced by any stop_times.stop_id under this check.

Operational effect

Why it matters

It may be a stale record, an omitted timetable connection, or a location used indirectly.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Search for the stop in stop_times.txt and check relevant station, pathway, and flexible-service relationships.

  2. 2

    Restore a missing reference if the stop is served. Remove it only after confirming that it is obsolete and unneeded elsewhere; do not delete station infrastructure just because no vehicle stops there.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
