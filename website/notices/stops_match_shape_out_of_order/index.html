Diagnosis

What this notice means

Stops appear in one order in stop_times.txt but match the shape in another order.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Map and journey information may follow the wrong direction or wrong pass through a loop.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Compare the two reported stops, trip stop_sequence, shape direction, and any distance values.

  2. 2

    Correct the wrong stop order or shape assignment. For loops, use cumulative distances to distinguish repeated visits without reordering a correct timetable.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
