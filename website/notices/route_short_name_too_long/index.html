Diagnosis

What this notice means

Short name of a route is too long (more than 12 characters).

Operational effect

Why it matters

Long route labels are truncated or wrap badly in compact signs, maps, and mobile interfaces.

Scope

Affected files and report fields

  • routes.txtOpen GTFS specification
Report fieldTypeMeaning
csvRowNumberintegerThe row number of the faulty record.
fieldNamestringContext included in the report.
filenamestringContext included in the report.
routeIdstringThe id of the faulty record.
routeShortNamestringThe faulty record's `route_short_name`.

Repair

How to fix it

Put the concise public route designator in route_short_name and move the descriptive text to route_long_name.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open routes.txt and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Put the concise public route designator in route_short_name and move the descriptive text to route_long_name.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that route_short_name_too_long no longer appears for the record.

Worked example

Bad row and correction

Before

route_id,route_short_name,route_long_name,route_type
R1,Airport Express,,3

After

route_id,route_short_name,route_long_name,route_type
R1,AX,Airport Express,3
