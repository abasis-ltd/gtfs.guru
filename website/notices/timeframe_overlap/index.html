Diagnosis

What this notice means

Rows in timeframes.txt with the same timeframe_group_id and service_id cover overlapping times.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The fare calendar contains overlapping definitions for the same group.

Scope

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Compare the reported current and previous rows and their boundary times.

  2. 2

    Merge redundant intervals or adjust them to the real fare periods. An interval may end exactly when the next begins, because the end boundary is excluded.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.

Worked example

Example correction

Assume the second fare period really begins at 09:00. Adjacent periods may share that boundary.

Before

G1,08:30:00,10:00:00,WKDY

After

timeframe_group_id,start_time,end_time,service_id
G1,07:00:00,09:00:00,WKDY
G1,09:00:00,10:00:00,WKDY
