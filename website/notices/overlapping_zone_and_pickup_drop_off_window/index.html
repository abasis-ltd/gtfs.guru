Diagnosis

What this notice means

Within a trip, two service areas overlap geographically and have overlapping windows with the same pickup or drop-off type.

Operational effect

Why it matters

An app may have multiple conflicting ways to interpret service at the same place and time.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Compare both reported locations, windows, and stop sequences in stop_times.txt and locations.geojson.

  2. 2

    Correct accidental polygon overlap or window/type settings. Merge duplicate service descriptions only when they represent the same actual service.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
