Diagnosis

What this notice means

The reported service has no remaining active dates after considering both the weekly calendar and date exceptions.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Trips using this service will not provide upcoming departures.

Scope

Files to check and report fields

  • calendar.txtOpen GTFS specification
  • calendar_dates.txtOpen GTFS specification
Report fieldTypeMeaning
csvRowNumberintegerThe row of the faulty record.
serviceIdstringThe service id of the faulty record.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Check the service in both calendar.txt and calendar_dates.txt against the intended publication period.

  2. 2

    Publish the confirmed new timetable, or remove retired service records and their dependent trips. Do not extend dates without confirming that the service still runs.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
