Diagnosis

What this notice means

Rows for a trip are not contiguous or do not appear in stop_sequence order.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Some tools process grouped, ordered records more efficiently; the notice does not mean the service sequence itself is necessarily wrong.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Check whether the data is correct when grouped by trip_id and sorted numerically by stop_sequence.

  2. 2

    Export in that order while keeping each row's values unchanged. Do not renumber a correct sequence merely to match an accidental file order.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
