Diagnosis

What this notice means

A pickup/drop-off window is present alongside drop_off_type=0, including an applicable default.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The row mixes a flexible service window with regularly scheduled drop-off.

Scope

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Check the reported row in stop_times.txt and the actual drop-off arrangement.

  2. 2

    For a window-based service, choose an allowed drop-off type that matches the service. If drop-off is scheduled, remove the inappropriate window and provide the required times.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
