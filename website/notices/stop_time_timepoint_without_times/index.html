Diagnosis

What this notice means

Any records with stop_times.timepoint set to 1 must define a value for stop_times.arrival_time and stop_times.departure_time fields.

Operational effect

Why it matters

This is a GTFS requirement violation. Consumers may reject the affected data or interpret it incorrectly.
