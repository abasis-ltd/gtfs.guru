Diagnosis

What this notice means

The final stop's cumulative distance exceeds the shape's maximum, but the endpoints are within the implementation's 11.1 m tolerance.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Rounding or inconsistent distance calculations may prevent reliable stop placement.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Compare the maximum distance values, units, and endpoint coordinates in the reported trip and shape.

  2. 2

    Recalculate with a shared distance origin and sufficient precision. Preserve the real geometry rather than shifting coordinates to match a rounded number.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
