Diagnosis

What this notice means

The modeled pathways do not provide a required direction of travel between the reported location and station entrances or exits.

Operational effect

Why it matters

A journey planner may fail to find a usable route through the station.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Check hasEntrance, hasExit, the station map, and all pathway directions around the location.

  2. 2

    Add missing real links or correct reversed endpoints and bidirectionality. Do not make an exit gate or other one-way connection bidirectional unless it truly is.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
