Diagnosis

What this notice means

Referenced locations (using stop_times.stop_id) must be stops/platforms, i.e. their stops.location_type value must be 0 or empty.

Operational effect

Why it matters

This is a GTFS requirement violation. Consumers may reject the affected data or interpret it incorrectly.
