Diagnosis

What this notice means

A location that requires stop_name has an empty name.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Passengers may see an unlabeled boarding point, station, or entrance.

Scope

Files to check and report fields

  • stops.txtOpen GTFS specification
Report fieldTypeMeaning
csvRowNumberintegerThe row of the faulty record.
locationTypestring`stops.location_type` of the faulty record.
stopIdstringThe `stops.stop_id` of the faulty record.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Find the reported stopId in stops.txt and confirm its location type.

  2. 2

    Add the real passenger-facing name. For an entrance, use a recognizable entrance designation; do not substitute an internal ID unless that is genuinely its public name.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
