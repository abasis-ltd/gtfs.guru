Diagnosis

What this notice means

Consecutive points have different coordinates but equal cumulative distances, with a small geographic separation.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Rounding may make it difficult to interpolate accurately between the points.

Scope

Files to check and report fields

  • shapes.txtOpen GTFS specification
Report fieldTypeMeaning
This notice has no structured context fields.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Check the coordinates and distance precision in the two reported shapes.txt rows.

  2. 2

    Recalculate distances with enough decimal places, or remove a redundant point if doing so preserves the actual path.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
