Diagnosis

What this notice means

An identifier contains non-ASCII or non-printable characters.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The ID may be difficult to copy, compare, or use across systems.

Scope

Files to check and report fields

Use the file or input resource identified in the report. Check the message and available context fields; runtime failures may not identify a GTFS row.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Inspect the exact characters in the reported ID, not just how it looks on screen.

  2. 2

    Remove unintended invisible characters. If replacing an ID with a portable alternative, update every reference consistently; do not remove accented characters from passenger names in response to this ID-specific warning.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
