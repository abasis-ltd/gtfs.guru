Diagnosis

What this notice means

fast_travel_between_far_stops is a warning in a GTFS Schedule validation report: travel between distant stops appears too fast. Check routes.txt, stop_times.txt, stops.txt and trips.txt.

Two stops more than 10 km apart imply a speed above the threshold for the route's transport type.

Operational effect

Why it matters

A distant wrong stop or missing hour can distort the trip's journey time substantially.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Use the reported IDs, distance, times, and speed to inspect the pair on a map and in the timetable.

  2. 2

    Correct wrong references, coordinates, times, or route type. Check the service-day date before changing an overnight journey.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
