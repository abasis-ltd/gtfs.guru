ERRORGTFS Schedule notice

A pathway has an endpoint that is a stop with stop_access=1, which should be accessible outside of the station’s pathways.

Diagnosis

What this notice means

Stops with stop_access=1 should not have pathways that link to them since they can be accessed from the street and are independent of the pathways inside the station.

Operational effect

Why it matters

This is a GTFS requirement violation. Consumers may reject the affected data or interpret it incorrectly.
