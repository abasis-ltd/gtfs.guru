Diagnosis

What this notice means

The value is numeric, but it is not allowed for the reported field or type.

Operational effect

Why it matters

A coordinate, count, or setting may be impossible or interpreted incorrectly.

Scope

Files to check and report fields

Use the file or input resource identified in the report. Check the message and available context fields; runtime failures may not identify a GTFS row.

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Check fieldName, fieldType, and fieldValue against the field's allowed range.

  2. 2

    Restore the correct source value and units. Do not clamp an incorrect coordinate or count to the nearest boundary merely to make it pass.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
