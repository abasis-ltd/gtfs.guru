Diagnosis

What this notice means

A stop ID in transfers.txt refers to a location type other than stop/platform (0) or station (1).

Operational effect

Why it matters

The transfer uses an endpoint type that this rule does not allow.

Scope

Repair

How to fix it

  1. 1

    Use stopIdFieldName and stopId to inspect the endpoint in stops.txt.

  2. 2

    Reference the correct stop/platform or station. Do not reclassify an entrance or boarding area solely to make the transfer valid.

  3. 3
    Check the result

    After a repair, rebuild the archive and validate it again. Confirm that the affected records are correct and that no new related notices appeared. A reviewed warning or informational notice may remain when it describes intentional, supported data.
