Diagnosis

What this notice means

This field contains customer-facing text and should use Mixed Case (upper and lower case letters) to ensure good readability when displayed to riders. Avoid the use of abbreviations throughout the feed (e.g. St. for Street) unless a location is called by its abbreviated name (e.g. “JFK Airport”). Abbreviations may be problematic for accessibility by screen reader software and voice user interfaces.

Operational effect

Why it matters

ALL-CAPS or all-lowercase customer text is harder to scan and performs poorly in screen readers and voice interfaces.

Repair

How to fix it

Rewrite rider-facing names in natural mixed case while preserving official capitalization and acronyms.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open the file named in the notice and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Rewrite rider-facing names in natural mixed case while preserving official capitalization and acronyms.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that mixed_case_recommended_field no longer appears for the record.
