Diagnosis

What this notice means

Best Practices for feed_info.txt suggest providing at least one of feed_contact_email and feed_contact_url.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Feed consumers have no reliable operational contact when they find a data problem.

Scope

Affected files and report fields

The affected file is determined at validation time. Use the filename or related context field in your report to locate it.

Report fieldTypeMeaning
csvRowNumberintegerThe row number of the validated record.

Repair

How to fix it

Add feed_contact_email, feed_contact_url, or both to feed_info.txt and keep the contact monitored.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open feed_info.txt and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Add feed_contact_email, feed_contact_url, or both to feed_info.txt and keep the contact monitored.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that missing_feed_contact_email_and_url no longer appears for the record.

Worked example

Bad row and correction

Before

feed_publisher_name,feed_publisher_url,feed_lang
Metro,https://example.com,en

After

feed_publisher_name,feed_publisher_url,feed_lang,feed_contact_email
Metro,https://example.com,en,gtfs@example.com
