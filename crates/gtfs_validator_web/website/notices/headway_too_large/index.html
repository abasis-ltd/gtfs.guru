Diagnosis

What this notice means

A frequencies.txt row has headway_secs greater than 3600. Confirm that the value is expressed in seconds and that frequency-based service is the intended model.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The feed may still load, but correcting this warning improves data quality and the rider experience.
